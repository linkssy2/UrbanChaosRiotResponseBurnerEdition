Spanish text for Urban Chaos: Riot Response - Burner Edition

Community translation: ZARF, "Traduccion Beta Urban Chaos RRX".
Missing-text supplements and PC-menu translations: Burner Edition project.

This included bundle contains translated string changes only. Setup combines
them with the user's imported Xbox ISO files on the first Play after import or
upgrade. No separate Spanish download or installation is needed.
Select Spanish in Setup to use the Spanish text and translated PC menus.

The original English voices and other language banks are preserved. Spanish
translation remains a community beta and may still need wording corrections.
